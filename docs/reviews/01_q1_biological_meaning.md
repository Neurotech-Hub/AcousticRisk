# Review: 01_q1_biological_meaning.tex

## Assessment

The section answers its organizing question rather than cataloguing studies: it builds toward a concrete standard for an acoustic control and respects the key guardrails (naturalness neither necessary nor sufficient; Parsana as a frequency code; prairie dog calls as sufficient information rather than labels; Mongeau as a non-vocal boundary case). The laboratory paragraphs are tight and accurate to the map. The weaker parts are the field paragraph, which drifts toward a list, and a few inferential steps that go further than the cited evidence.

## Changes made

- Paragraph 2 (simplified calls): cut the formulaic "Taken together".
- Paragraph 3 (intensity): replaced "resembles no rodent call" with the map's "not a natural call". The original was unsupported, and a 17–20 kHz sweep lies near the rat 22-kHz band.
- Paragraph 4 (Schiavo): deleted the closing clause saying a synthetic sound "can carry the same information as a natural one". It overstated the result (tuning broadened) and restated the topic sentence.
- Paragraph 5 (marmots): changed "other acoustic parameters" to "some", matching the map.
- Paragraph 5 (Blumstein & Récapet): stated the comparison (more than unmanipulated calls), taken from the evidence matrix, and added a `% VERIFY` for it.
- Paragraph 6: moved the SchmidtEtAl2008 `% VERIFY` from the end of a prose line onto its own line. The marker text is unchanged.

## Needs author decision

1. **Location:** Paragraph 3, "Significance and intensity thus interact".
   **Issue:** At 100 dB, the acoustic differences between the squeal and the burst-timing-matched mimic may simply become easier to detect. An interaction with level does not by itself show that *significance* is the interacting factor.
   **Recommendation:** State this alternative in one clause, or soften "significance" to "the natural-call advantage".
2. **Location:** Paragraph 2, "carried by frequency band and gross temporal structure … modulation adds to, rather than creates, the response".
   **Issue:** None of the cited studies isolates gross temporal structure. "Adds to" rests on Olszyński, but the natural calls and tones there differ in more than modulation.
   **Recommendation:** Restrict the claim to what Wöhr & Schwarting and Parsana show (frequency band suffices for much of the effect; modulation is not necessary).
3. **Location:** Paragraph 7, "Laboratory studies have approached this standard for rat ultrasonic calls and mouse pup calls".
   **Issue:** Only Evans & Hammond varied sound level, and none of the rat ultrasonic-call studies cited here tested more than one level with experience controlled.
   **Recommendation:** Say which elements of the standard each system meets, or say "partly approached".
4. **Location:** Paragraph 6, opening "independently of call structure".
   **Issue:** Caller identity and caller class are themselves encoded in acoustic structure, so this phrasing conflicts with the section's own feature-based framing.
   **Recommendation:** Rephrase, for example as "independently of call type".
5. **Location:** Paragraph 1 and the section as a whole.
   **Issue:** The opening names novelty as a confound, and the outline asks Q1 to separate novelty and to use the smoke-detector (error-management) framing. Neither is taken up later.
   **Recommendation:** Either drop novelty from the opening or add one sentence linking it to habituation (Q6). Consider one sentence on why receivers should tolerate false alarms to crude acoustic cues.
6. **Location:** Paragraph 5, the Sloan et al. and Swan & Hare sentence.
   **Issue:** This sentence names no acoustic dimension, so it reads as a list entry inside an argument about dimensions.
   **Recommendation:** Name the feature (call type, syllable position) as information about risk, or merge it with the marmot sentence.
7. **Repetition across sections:**
   - Schmidt chipmunk result: Q1 paragraph 6 and Q2 (line 47, already cross-referenced).
   - Wöhr & Schwarting and Parsana: Q1 paragraph 2 and Synthesis paragraph 2 (near-identical wording).
   - Prairie dog escape: Q1 paragraph 5 and Q4 paragraph 2 (acceptable division of detail).

   Decide where each full statement lives.
