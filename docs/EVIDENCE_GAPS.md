# Evidence-gap register

Each row: proposed application or synthetic design feature → existing evidence → unresolved question → decisive validation experiment. Companion to the revision’s §6 and computational agenda.

| Application / design feature | Existing evidence | Unresolved question | Decisive validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic reconstruction matching frequency, level, envelope, modulation | Wöhr sine approx. approach; Parsana tone-matched amygdala; Evans squeal vs mimic at 2 SPLs | When do residual natural advantages remain after full matching? | Natural vs matched synthetic vs noise on a declared endpoint (§6 exp. 1) |
| Significance slows habituation / speeds recovery | Evans recovery asymmetry at 100 dB; Berz: meaningful 50-kHz still habituates | Does significance change *rate* vs matched controls under identical schedules? | Natural / synthetic / noise × schedules; recovery function (§6 exp. 2) |
| Irregular / hazard-based scheduling for natural calls | Davis & Herry: irregular preserves responding to *artificial* sounds | Do interval *distributions* with natural calls preserve field/lab endpoints? | Matched count & mean ISI; fixed vs uniform vs exponential with natural calls |
| Lab caller-reliability learning | Identity in rats/voles; reliability only in sciurids (discount vs scrutinize) | Do lab rodents discount or scrutinize unreliable callers? | Pair/unpair callers with outcomes; optional checking-cost manipulation (§6 exp. 3) |
| Accumbens vs tail-striatum DA to ecological threat calls | Opposite regional responses to noise; 50-kHz raises NAc DA; 22-kHz does not | Do alarm/predator calls reproduce noise-like regional patterns? | Dual-site recording during ecological + control sounds (§6 exp. 4) |
| Field deterrence with predator vocalizations | Conflict in naïve lab rodents; field-experienced responses vary by species | Which species/contexts show durable escape or intake change? | Endpoint-specific field tests with non-predator controls; persistence metric |
| Heterospecific alarms for vigilance induction | Shriner, Getschow, Waterman & Mai, Schmidt | Transfer when familiarity and acoustic similarity are dissociated? | Crossed familiarity × similarity designs |
| Safety-cue playback to speed recovery | Lilly: chatter after hawk call | Can engineered “all-clear” acoustics match chatter? | Synthetic safety morphs vs natural chatter vs noise |
| Graded vigilance via call rate / nonlinearity | Blumstein rate; Blumstein & Récapet nonlinearity | Anti-habituation claim for nonlinearity under repetition? | Repeated exposure with/without nonlinear components |
| Lab–field translation of same materials | Baack, Jayne foraging costs; lab assays often omit costs | Do lab effects survive when foraging/refuge costs are restored? | Same acoustics in field vs lab ± cost structure (§6 exp. 5) |
| Risk allocation with acoustic cues | Lima & Bednekoff theory only | Does vigilance per alarm fall as alarm frequency rises? | Field regime manipulation with caller held constant (§6 exp. 6) |
| Ultrasonic playback in field devices | Lab 22/50-kHz work; field speakers usually audible-band | Can calibrated ultrasonic field playback reproduce lab endpoints? | Hardware + calibration + species hearing check before efficacy claims |
| Programmable field schedulers (e.g. ACORN-class) | Framework application only; no efficacy claim in this review | Which schedules preserve maintained response under matched exposure? | Pre-registered comparison of schedules with natural references |
| Cross-species transfer of optimized features | None demonstrated here | Do features optimized in rats transfer to mice or sciurids? | Same feature vector; species-specific endpoints and hearing |
