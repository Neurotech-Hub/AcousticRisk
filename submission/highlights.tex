{\large Interpreting Acoustic Risk: Integrating Field Ethology and Laboratory Neuroscience in Rodents}\\[0.35em]
{\normalsize Matt Gaidica}
\end{center}
\vspace{1.25em}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Field and laboratory studies of rodent alarm sounds developed largely apart
  \item A receiver-centered framework separates rapid defense, learning, and action choice
  \item Recognizing a threat and acting on it are separable; behavior alone can mislead
  \item Identical response declines can reflect habituation, reliability, timing, or cost
  \item Circuits mediating sciurid alarm-call responses have not been causally established
\end{itemize}
